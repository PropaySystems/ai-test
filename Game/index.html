How to play

Objective

Win by capturing all 12 of your opponent's pieces or leaving your opponent without a valid move.

Setup

  1. The game is played by two players on an 8×8 board.
  2. One player controls 12 black pieces; the other controls 12 white pieces.
  3. Pieces begin on the dark squares of the first three rows at each end.
  4. Black takes the first turn.

Taking a turn

  1. Select one of your pieces, then select its destination.
  2. A normal piece moves one square diagonally forward onto an empty dark square.
  3. Only one normal move may be made per turn.
  4. Play then passes to the other player.

Capturing

  1. Capture by jumping diagonally over an adjacent opponent piece into the empty square immediately behind it.
  2. The jumped piece is removed from the board immediately.
  3. A normal piece may only jump forward.
  4. If any of your pieces can capture, you must make a capture.
  5. If the capturing piece can jump again after landing, it must continue jumping until no further capture is available.

Kings

  1. A normal piece becomes a king immediately upon reaching the last row on the opposite side of the board.
  2. Kings are marked with the word “KING”.
  3. Kings may move and capture diagonally both forward and backward.
  4. Kings still move one square normally and jump one adjacent piece at a time.

Ending the game

The game ends immediately when a player captures their opponent's final piece or leaves the opponent without a valid move. In either case, the other player wins. The winner is announced and celebrated with confetti. Use Reset game to start again.
