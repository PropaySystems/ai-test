📖 How to Play

🎯 Goal

Be the last commander standing. You win when you capture all opponent pieces, or leave them with no legal move.

🪙 The Toss

A coin toss decides who plays Black. Black always moves first.

♟ Moving Men

  • Pieces live only on the dark squares.
  • A man moves diagonally forward one square to an empty dark square.
  • Men cannot move backward or sideways.

⚔ Capturing

  • Jump diagonally over an adjacent enemy onto the empty square just beyond it.
  • Captures are forced — if a jump is available you must take it.
  • Multi-jumps: if another capture is possible from the landing square, you must keep jumping in the same turn.

👑 Kinging

  • Reach the opponent's back row and your man is crowned a King.
  • Kings move and capture forward and backward, one square at a time.
  • Being crowned mid-jump ends your turn.

🏆 Winning

  • Capture every enemy piece, or block them so they have no legal move.

🎨 On-board Cues

  • Blue ring = selected piece · Blue dot = valid move.
  • Gold ring = a piece that must capture · Red ring = capture landing square.
  • Gold outline = last move played.
