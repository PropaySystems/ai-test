How to play

  • Drag a piece or click, then drop/click a highlighted square.
  • Captures are mandatory — if you can jump, you must.
  • Keep jumping with the same piece when another jump opens.
  • Reach the far row to crown a king.
  • Use Suggest move for one recommended play.

Keyboard still works: Tab to a square, Enter or Space to act.
