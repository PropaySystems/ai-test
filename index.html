Rules reminder

  • Only dark squares are playable.
  • Men move diagonally forward one square.
  • Captures are mandatory — if you can jump, you must.
  • Continue jumping with the same piece when another jump opens.
  • Reach the far row to become a king (moves both ways).
  • Win by capturing all pieces or leaving no legal moves.

Tip: click a piece, then a highlighted square. Keyboard users can Tab to squares and press Enter or Space.
