Dots Board

Two players, 12 dots each on black squares. Men move diagonally forward; kings move diagonally both ways. Capture by jumping over an enemy onto the empty black square behind it.

Men move one diagonal step forward on black squares. Kings (gold ring) move diagonally forward or back. To capture, jump over an enemy dot onto the empty black square right behind it — you may chain jumps. Reach the far row to become a king. Win by capturing all enemy dots or leaving them with no moves.
